PRIVACY POLICY

Your documents stay yours.

Effective date: September 29, 2026

PDF Scanner is designed so its core document scanning, OCR, search, organization, signature, and PDF export workflows operate on your device.

Information processed on your device

  • Camera images and photos you choose to scan or import.
  • PDF files you explicitly import.
  • Recognized document text, page filters, rotations, folders, favorites, and document names.
  • A reusable signature if you choose to save one.
  • App preferences needed to remember settings such as PDF export quality.

Core document content is stored in the app's protected local storage and is not uploaded to the developer as part of normal scanning, OCR, search, signing, or PDF creation. Your device and backup settings may separately include app data.

Cloud document intelligence

If a release offers connected cloud-AI features, using them is optional. Before a request is sent, the app shows the recognized text that will be submitted. The request sends selected recognized text and the question or instruction you provide; raw scanned images are not the default cloud-AI payload. Cloud processing may involve a service provider and is subject to the retention and security practices disclosed for that connected release. You can decline the request and continue using local scanning features.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. When RevenueCat is configured for a release, it may process purchase, product, device, and anonymous customer-identification information needed to determine subscription entitlements. PDF Scanner does not send your scanned document content to RevenueCat.

Sharing and exports

When you choose to share, export, print, or save a PDF to another service, the destination you choose receives that file. A signed export includes the signature you placed on the document. Third-party destinations operate under their own privacy policies.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app does not include advertising and does not use App Tracking Transparency for cross-app advertising tracking. The current release does not send product analytics events to the developer. We do not sell scanned documents or personal information.

Retention and deletion

Documents remain in local app storage until you delete them or remove the app, subject to normal iOS/iPadOS backup behavior. You can delete documents in the app and remove the reusable signature in Settings. Temporary PDF previews are periodically cleaned up. Copies you exported are controlled by the destination where you saved or shared them. Apple purchase records remain subject to Apple's retention practices. For questions about deletion of any server-side information associated with an optional connected service, contact support.

Your choices

You can deny camera access, avoid cloud-AI features, delete local documents, remove your saved signature, stop using subscription features, and uninstall the app. You may also manage App Store subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact beenken@gmail.com or visit the support page. Do not send sensitive document contents unless they are necessary to resolve your request.
