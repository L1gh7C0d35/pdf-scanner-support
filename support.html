How can we help?

Quick fixes

  • If scanning is unavailable, allow Camera access in iPhone or iPad Settings.
  • For difficult pages, improve lighting, reduce glare, and keep the document flat and sharp.
  • Use Enhanced, Grayscale, or Black & White during review if the original page is hard to read.
  • For the clearest exported PDF, go to Settings > PDF export and choose Best. Small creates smaller files at reduced resolution. Increasing export quality cannot restore detail missing from the original scan.
  • If a page already looks blurry while reviewing it, tap Retake page; use brighter, even light, avoid glare, and hold the phone steady. For difficult fine print, try Best Shot from Library > Import.
  • Version 1.0 is free: scanning, OCR search, signing, and PDF export do not require a subscription or purchase restore.

Contact support

Email beenken@gmail.com or open a support request on GitHub.

Include your iOS/iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. Do not email or publicly post sensitive documents, signatures, account numbers, purchase receipts, or private scanned text.
